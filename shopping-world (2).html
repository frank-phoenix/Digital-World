📋 How To Buy Online
1
🌐
Open the Website
Go to a shopping site like amazon.co.uk on your phone or computer
2
🔍
Search for It
Type the name in the search bar — like "blue jeans" or "headphones"
3
👀
Look at the Results
See pictures, names and prices — tap to find out more!
4
✅
Choose Your Item
Check the size, colour and price — is it right for you?
5
🛒
Add to Basket
Press "Add to Basket" — your item is saved in your cart!
6
💳
Go to Checkout
Click your basket → "Checkout" → "Buy Now" to pay
7
🏠
Enter Your Address
Tell them where to deliver — type your home address carefully!
8
💰
Pay!
Use a card or PayPal — always ask a trusted adult to help! 👩‍👦
9
🚚
Wait for Delivery!
Your parcel is coming! Track it on the app — usually 1–5 days 📬

🔒 Stay Safe Online!
🛡️
Your Safety Comes First!
Always ask a trusted adult before buying anything online!
🔑
Keep Passwords Secret!
Never tell anyone your password — not even friends!
👩‍👦
Ask a Grown Up!
Always ask a parent or carer before buying anything!
🔒
Look for the Padlock!
A padlock in the browser bar means the website is safe!
✅
Real Websites Only!
Check the website address is correct before buying!
⭐
Read Reviews!
Check what other shoppers say — stars and comments help you choose!
💰
Check the Price!
Too cheap? It might be fake! Be careful!
🏠
Keep Address Private!
Only give your address to trusted shopping websites!
